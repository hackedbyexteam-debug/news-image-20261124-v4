One-line entry for make.fun — Sum up 24 November's world news in one image bounty, $100 in USDC.

A 1024×1024 broadsheet of Tuesday 24 November 2026: **70 stories in depth, from 8 regions around the world**, all first reported that day. Three illustrated lead stories with full summaries and WHY IT MATTERS, ten more stories with drawings and fuller text, a UTC timeline of the day, a world map with numbered dots, and the remaining stories numbered in columns. A DATE CHECK footer names what was left out and why, and every story carries an honest status tag: OFFICIAL (an authority's own word), REPORTED (press report) or CLAIM (one side's say). Every drawing is original, and every figure was checked against the day's reporting.

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to the output directory, where the gate reads it.
